# Licensing status / 许可状态

This is a local review draft. No repository-wide open licence has been selected or granted by this document. Before publication, the researcher should select an appropriate licence for newly written code and approve attribution for newly authored prose/data summaries. The exact selection is pending review; do not label the whole package MIT, CC0 or open geodata.

Third-party news, historical screenshots, map data, imagery, photographs, transcripts and hand-drawn work retain their respective rights. Public accessibility does not grant redistribution rights. This package contains links and original short summaries rather than copies of the third-party article or image assets. Descriptive metadata is not a transfer of rights to originals.

No OSM geometry or tiles have been exported into this package. Future OSM-derived data requires the applicable ODbL attribution/conditions; tiles and third-party imagery have additional provider rules. A code licence cannot relicense those assets. Future Leaflet inclusion needs its own licence notice.

The hand-drawn map and field photographs are excluded pending authorship, consent, de-identification and reuse review. No consent or photo ownership is inferred from a previous publication or archive manifest. See [methods](docs/methods.md) and [release review](docs/review-before-release.md).
